## How it works

There is no login area for now — deliberately: we don't keep passwords or
personal data in a system that isn't needed yet. Instead:

- every member gets their **statement by email**, whenever something changes;
- data changes are requested by email or form and handled by a human being;
- the member list is private and never shared with third parties.

When the association grows enough to justify a real login area, it will appear
here — under the same privacy rules.

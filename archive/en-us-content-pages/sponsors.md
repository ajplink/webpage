## Support tiers

- **Friend of Plink** — one-off support for an event or activity. Appears in our thanks and in our hearts.
- **Silver support** — annual support for a project. Logo on the site and on the project's materials.
- **Gold support** — annual partner of the association. Logo everywhere, presence at events, and an impact report on what you funded.

Tiers are conversations, not catalogues: every arrangement is shaped around
what makes sense for both sides.

## What you get as a member

- **A real say** — members vote in the assembly and set the direction of the association.
- **Project support** — submit your own projects, with a mentor and access to our resources.
- **Borrowable kit** — tools, kits and instruments for your own activities.
- **Events and workshops** — guaranteed place, discounted sign-ups.
- **Training and mentoring** — learn from people who have done it, teach people starting out.
- **A whole network** — schools, educators and young people who like making things seriously.

## How to become a member

1. Fill in the membership form — it takes two minutes.
2. Pay the annual fee (figures below).
3. Get your confirmation and membership number by email.

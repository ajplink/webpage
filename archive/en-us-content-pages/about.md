> Science isn't a display case. It's something you build with your hands, that
> fails on the third try and works on the fourth. That's what we bring to schools.

## What we do

Associação Plink creates hands-on science, technology, engineering and maths
projects for young people in Portugal. We work with schools, educators and
volunteers to build practical, inclusive, accessible experiences — kits that
get assembled, workshops that get messy, competitions that get won (and lost)
with good sportsmanship.

## What we believe

- **Learning by doing** — every project starts from a real question and ends with something built.
- **Access for all** — activities that work in any school, on any budget.
- **Open science** — materials and methods shared, so others can reuse and improve them.
- **Community** — young people, teachers and volunteers working side by side, without unnecessary hierarchy.

## How it works

The association is organised in departments, each with its own specialty —
from building materials to event logistics. Any member can submit a project:
the approved ones get a mentor and access to our resources.

And yes, everything is decided in meetings. But meetings with minutes — that
end on time.

## Where the name comes from

*Plink* is the sound of an idea landing right: the click, the bell, the raindrop
on the window. We chose a name that is written in a single stroke — and a
lightbulb that never goes out.

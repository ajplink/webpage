## Mission

Associação Plink facilitates hands-on STEM learning projects for young people in Portugal. We work with schools, educators and volunteers to create practical, inclusive and accessible experiences.

## Values

- **Learning by doing** — every project starts from a real question and ends with something built.
- **Access for all** — activities designed to work in any school, on any budget.
- **Open science** — shared materials and methods so others can reuse and improve them.
- **Community** — young people, teachers and volunteers working side by side.

## How it works

The association is organised in four departments:

1. **DREE** — Educational Resources: materials, guides and teacher training.
2. **DP** — Projects: support for member-submitted projects.
3. **DL** — Logistics: events, workshops and competitions.
4. **DCI** — Communication & IT: website, social media and internal tools.

Any member can submit a project. Approved projects get a mentor and access to the association's resources.
